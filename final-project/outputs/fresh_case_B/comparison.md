## How to read this table

- **Peak monthly outflow** includes interest, principal and any monthly fee. For `equal_principal` offers the outflow declines over time, so the peak (first month) is the binding affordability number.
- **All-in cost** = total interest + upfront fee + all monthly fees. It is a nominal HKD amount and is *not* adjusted for the timing of cash flows.
- **Effective APR / EAR** come from the IRR of the borrower's actual cash flows (net disbursement at t=0, instalments at t=1..n). They are the only figures here that put fees, rates and terms on one comparable footing.
- **Affordable** means the peak instalment fits both the monthly budget cap and the payment-to-income ceiling.

## Limitations

Monthly instalments only; no compounding within a month, no early repayment, no variable rates, no default risk, no tax effects and no time value applied to the nominal all-in-cost figure. Fees are assumed to be withheld from the disbursement. Offers are synthetic teaching data, not real quotes. See `SKILL.md` and `data/source_notes.md`.
